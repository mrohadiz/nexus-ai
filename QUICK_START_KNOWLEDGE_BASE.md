## 💡 Pro Tips for Better Results

### 1. Be Specific & Detailed
❌ Bad: "Saya bingung soal strategy"  
✅ Good: "Saya torn antara dua options: (1) focus pada customer retention dengan improve product quality, atau (2) aggressive acquisition dengan marketing spend increase. Here's my thinking on each..."

### 2. Share Your Reasoning Process
Don't just share conclusions. Share HOW you think:
```
"When I face this decision, my instinct is to [X]. Then I usually 
consider [Y factors]. But I often struggle with [Z trade-off]. 
Here's why I'm leaning toward option A..."
```

### 3. Challenge Your Own Assumptions
```
"I believe that [assumption]. But I'm not 100% sure. Can you help 
me test this assumption? What evidence would prove it wrong?"
```

### 4. Reflect on Past Decisions
```
"3 bulan lalu saya decide to [decision]. Here's what happened... 
Looking back, I think I made the right/wrong choice because... 
What does this reveal about my decision-making style?"
```

### 5. Ask for Pattern Analysis Regularly
Every 5-10 conversations, ask:
```
"Based on our last 10 conversations, what are 3 recurring patterns 
in how I think about business problems?"
```

## 🚨 Common Mistakes to Avoid

### ❌ Mistake 1: Too Generic
"I want to grow my business" → Too vague  
✅ Fix: "I'm trying to decide between investing in product development vs marketing spend. Here's my current situation..."

### ❌ Mistake 2: No Context
"What should I do?" → AI can't help without context  
✅ Fix: "Here's my situation: [detailed context]. Given this, what factors should I consider?"

### ❌ Mistake 3: Not Challenging AI
Accepting first answer → Misses deeper insights  
✅ Fix: "I disagree with point #2. Here's why... What do you think?"

### ❌ Mistake 4: Inconsistent Usage
Chat once a month → Not enough data  
✅ Fix: Minimum 2-3 conversations per week for meaningful patterns

## 🆘 Troubleshooting

### Problem: AI gives generic responses
**Solution:**
- Provide more specific context
- Ask follow-up questions
- Challenge generic advice
- Switch to different model (try Gemma 31B)

### Problem: Slow response times
**Solution:**
- Try different model (some are faster)
- Use during off-peak hours (early morning/late night)
- Shorten messages if very long
- Check OpenRouter status page

### Problem: AI doesn't remember previous conversations
**Solution:**
- Reference past discussions explicitly
- Summarize key points from previous chats
- Use same session ID for related conversations
- Export and re-import context if needed

### Problem: Not seeing patterns emerge
**Solution:**
- Increase conversation frequency
- Be more consistent in topics
- Explicitly ask for pattern analysis
- Review conversation history yourself
